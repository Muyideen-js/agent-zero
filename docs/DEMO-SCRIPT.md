# Demo script (about 4 minutes)

Have the app running with a fresh browser profile, and NearBlocks open in a second tab.

## 0:00 Opening (landing page)
> "AI agents can already do real work. What they can't do safely is run a business: hold a budget, pay for work, sell, and stay inside rules. Agent Zero gives AI a company, a workforce and a treasury whose rules are enforced by a NEAR smart contract, not by the model."

Scroll slowly through the hero diagram and the "How it works" section.

## 0:30 Create a company
Open the app → **Create a company**.
- Name: `Lagos Pulse Insights`
- Mission: research AI bookkeeping tools for small businesses in Lagos and produce a report founders will pay for
- Treasury 3 NEAR · max 1.5 per payment · cap 5 · minimum price 4 · 80/20

> "This writes the company, its policy and five agent accounts to the contract."

Press **Create company**; show the transaction links.

## 1:00 The CEO plans
**Task market → Run autopilot.**
> "Atlas, the CEO, reads the mission and policy from the chain, plans the work, and registers every task on-chain against the right agent. Budgets are clamped to the policy before anything is created."

Click an **on-chain** link on a task card to show the `create_task` transaction.

## 1:45 The workforce delivers
As cards move to **Delivered**, open a deliverable.
> "Meridian wrote the research brief; Forge turned it into a customer-facing report."

Open **Products** and show the report and Download.

## 2:30 Approve and pay
Back in Task market: **Approve on-chain**, then **Request payout**.
> "The contract checked eight rules and paid the agent's account."

Click **View transaction on NearBlocks**.

## 3:00 The attack
**Treasury & security → Run attack on-chain.**
> "Now the Sales agent tries to pay itself more than the limit."

The checks animate and stop at **Within per-payment limit**.
> "Rejected by the contract. Funds didn't move, and the attempt is recorded on-chain as evidence."

Show the rejection in the ledger, then **Pause all payments** and show the PAUSED state.

## 3:40 Close
> "Agents do the work. NEAR enforces the money. Every balance on screen is read from the chain. Agent Zero: give AI a company, give it a treasury, let it build."
